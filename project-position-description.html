Position Description Revitalization

Multicultural workforce across regional and country offices

Organization
World Health Organization, South-East Asia Regional Office (SEARO)
Scope
Multicultural workforce across regional and country offices
Role
Lead, Position Description Standardization

Background & Context

Over time, position descriptions (PDs) across a large, multi-office organisation like WHO SEARO tend to drift out of sync with actual role responsibilities. Descriptions written years earlier no longer reflected evolving organisational priorities, new competency expectations, or shifts in how roles were actually being performed day to day. This created downstream problems: unclear expectations for staff, difficulty recruiting against outdated criteria, inconsistent classification, and a workforce whose documented capabilities did not clearly map to the organisation's current priority functions. This project was initiated to close that gap through a systematic revision and standardization effort.

Project Aim

To revise and standardize position descriptions across the organisation, strengthening role clarity, workforce-capability fit, and alignment with priority organisational functions across a multicultural, multi-office workforce.

My Involvement

I led the review and standardization effort, working directly with role owners, functional leads, and HR colleagues to ensure that every revised position description accurately reflected current responsibilities while conforming to a new, consistent organisational standard.

Key Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Audit of existing position descriptionsConducted a systematic review of existing PDs against current role realities, flagging descriptions that were outdated, inconsistent in format, or misaligned with the organisation's current priority functions.
  • Standardization framework developmentDesigned a standardized PD template and format covering role purpose, key responsibilities, required competencies, reporting lines, and alignment to organisational priority functions — ensuring consistency across every office and role level.
  • Stakeholder collaboration for content validationWorked closely with functional leads and role incumbents across a multicultural workforce spanning multiple offices to validate that revised PD content accurately reflected actual role responsibilities, through structured interviews and iterative review cycles.
  • Competency alignmentMapped each revised position description against the organisation's competency framework, ensuring documented required skills matched both the role's actual demands and strategic capability priorities.
  • Capability-fit analysisAssessed workforce-capability fit by comparing revised PDs against current staff competencies, surfacing areas where roles required capabilities not yet fully represented in the existing workforce.
  • Compliance and classification alignmentEnsured revised PDs met organisational compliance and job classification standards, supporting consistent grading and equitable treatment of similar roles across different offices.
  • Cross-cultural sensitivity in draftingAdapted language and framing of position descriptions to be clear and unambiguous across a multicultural, multilingual workforce, avoiding idioms or region-specific phrasing.
  • Rollout and documentationFinalized and distributed revised PDs to relevant stakeholders, along with documentation of the new standardized template and process for future PD updates.

Challenges Navigated

  • Scale and diversity of roles: standardizing PDs across a wide variety of functional areas and organisational levels required a flexible template that could accommodate diverse role types without losing consistency.
  • Balancing standardization with role-specific nuance: some roles had highly specialized responsibilities that resisted easy standardization, requiring care to preserve necessary detail.
  • Multicultural stakeholder alignment: coordinating validation input from functional leads across different cultural and linguistic contexts required patience and structured communication.

Results & Outcomes

  • Strengthened role clarity across the regional workforce, giving staff, managers, and recruiters a consistent, accurate reference for every position.
  • Improved alignment between staff capabilities and priority organisational functions, supporting more targeted recruitment, training, and performance management.
  • Created a standardized, reusable framework for future position description updates, reducing effort for subsequent revision cycles.
  • Improved consistency in job classification and compliance, reducing the risk of grading discrepancies across similar roles in different offices.
